BMW 330i scroll photo sequence

1. Open this folder in VS Code.
2. Run index.html with Live Server.
3. Scroll down: each viewport-height step switches to the next supplied photo with a short crossfade.

Photos are in images/angle-01.webp through angle-10.webp. No external libraries or internet connection are required.
This is a photo-sequence effect, not a real 3D model or continuous camera orbit.
